Step 1: Choose Planning Method

Select the method that will generate your initial planning quantities

1. Last Year Quantities

Copy previous year's monthly quantities directly.

April 2026 → April 2027
2. Sales Value Target

Enter monthly Sales Targets and Average Selling Price to calculate Qty.

Qty = Target / Price
3. Last Year + Growth %

Load last year's quantities and apply a growth % increase.

Qty = Last Year * (1 + Growth%)
4. Manual Qty Entry

Start with blank cells and enter monthly sales quantities manually.

Blank Sheet
5. Copy Existing Plan

Select another saved plan and copy all quantities and parameters.

Version iteration
6. Average of Past Years

System averages historical quantities of multiple preceding years.

Average (FY24, FY25, FY26)
7. Seasonality Planning

Enter Annual Quantity and distribute using monthly seasonality ratios.

April 7%, May 8%... (Sum = 100%)
8. Contribution Based

Input Total Planned Quantity and distribute by historical monthly share.

Distribute by prior year monthly %
9. Custom Formula

Define Growth%, adjustments, and add Festival / New Store targets.

Final = LY * (1+G%) + Adj + Fest + Shop
